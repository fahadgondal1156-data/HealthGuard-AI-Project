# TIHM Dataset Notice

HealthGuard-AI does not redistribute the TIHM dataset.

Official dataset: **TIHM: An open dataset for remote healthcare monitoring in dementia**, Zenodo record 7622128, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.7622128.

The official record states that the dataset is provided for research and patient-benefit purposes, says it cannot be used for commercial purposes, and requests acknowledgement of Surrey and Borders Partnership NHS Foundation Trust. Check the current official record before each new use and follow the applicable terms.

The associated Scientific Data article describes 56 participants, 2,803 participant-days and five tables: Activity, Sleep, Physiology, Labels and Demographics.

Do not upload raw TIHM records to a public repository.
